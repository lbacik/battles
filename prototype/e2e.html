End-to-end test: unit placement → draw ground cover → draw hills → save → reload → reopen → persistence step 6 → battle (Human vs Passive, two turns) → battle reload-resume

Drives the real domain model and the real IndexedDB scenario store in this browser: first the editor acceptance steps 5–7 (six units, a spacing preset, a rotation to 30°, a refused overlapping drop, accepted edge-to-edge touching, and a refused battlefield shrink), then the acceptance step 2 ground cover (forest, rough ground, a settlement, a refused overlapping area with a highlight ring, a clipped area), then the acceptance step 3 hill with two nested levels (levels 1 and 2 derived automatically) and a refused crossing hill with a highlight, then the river and road crossing the river and the forest (editing a vertex and a width on the way), then a save, a mid-run reload, and a reopen with identical values, then the persistence acceptance step 6 (import a file carrying a unit whose troop-type key is unknown to the catalogue, confirm the unit is marked and the issues panel warns, save it, and reopen with the key intact), and finally a battle that resolves a turn, saves to the battles collection, reloads mid-battle, and resumes the identical state before resolving another turn. Add ?autostart=1 to run on load.
